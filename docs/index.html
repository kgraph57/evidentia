Why now

Fabricated references are no longer rare

A Lancet audit (Topaz et al., 2026) of biomedical papers found fabricated references in 1 in 2,828 papers in 2023, 1 in 458 in 2025 (about sixfold), and 1 in 277 in the first 7 weeks of 2026 — about a 10-fold rise from 2023, in step with AI writing tools. A fabricated DOI looks exactly like a real one. Evidentia resolves each identifier and tells you which is which.

Topaz et al., Lancet 2026;407(10541):1779–1781. doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(26)00603-3 · STAT, 7 May 2026 · PMC open-access subset
4-tier classification

Not 'could not verify.' A reason.

Most citation checkers stop at a shrug. Evidentia resolves the identifier and says why a citation is suspect.

[OK] Verified

The paper exists and the cited title, authors, year, and journal match the registry record.

[REV] Content review needed

Registries cannot confirm it (book, guideline, title-only), or a real paper still needs a human or LLM to check whether it is used in the right context.

[MIS] Bibliographic mismatch

A real paper exists, but the DOI/PMID is wrong or the metadata disagrees — careless citation, or a fabricated identifier bolted onto a real title.

[HAL] Hallucination

The identifier resolves to nothing, or to a completely different paper. The signature of AI-generated text.

Two layers

A computer that does not guess, plus a skill that judges

Split on purpose: existence is deterministic. Honesty of use still needs judgment.

Engine · CLI + MCP

Does this paper exist?

Pure registry lookup. DOI, PMID, arXiv, NCT vs CrossRef, PubMed, OpenAlex, arXiv, ClinicalTrials.gov. No API key, no LLM, no hallucination of its own.

npx evidentia check · claude mcp add evidentia -- npx -y evidentia-mcp

Skill · 15 criteria

Is it used honestly?

Wraps the engine in a 15-criteria appraisal: evidence level, statistics, causation vs correlation, conflicts, exaggeration, population fit, ethics — an A–F report with concrete fixes. This is the Tier 2 layer the engine cannot do alone.

npx skills add kgraph57/evidentia
